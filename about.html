About Us | हमारे बारे में

Rooted in the philosophy of 'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam' (The World is One Family), we strive to build a global kinship through education, empowerment, and sustainable community development in India.

history_edu

Our Genesis हमारी शुरुआत

Founded in 2015 by a group of passionate educators and social workers in rural Rajasthan, Vasudhaiva Kulam began with a simple belief: every child deserves access to quality education, regardless of their socio-economic background.

What started as a small evening learning center under a banyan tree has now blossomed into a comprehensive NGO supporting thousands of families across three states, providing holistic development programs that bridge traditional wisdom with modern opportunities.
